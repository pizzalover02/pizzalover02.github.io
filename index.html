Background & Expertise

A decade of pushing AI boundaries

I'm a researcher obsessed with building AI systems that can reason, learn, and adapt in ways that push beyond current paradigms.

My journey began at MIT, where I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science focusing on attention mechanisms in deep neural networks. This work laid the foundation for modern transformer architectures and earned me the ACM Doctoral Dissertation Award.
